How Flintel rates a lead

Every rating comes from rules, not from a prediction of who will buy. Each lead shows the exact words the author wrote and a link to the original post.

  • High potential: the author is asking for what you sell (a request, a recommendation, a provider search or a buying statement), the post matches your offer closely, it is recent, and the location fits.
  • Relevant: the author describes a problem you solve, is comparing options or is weighing a price or a switch, and the post matches your offer closely.
  • Needs review: it could be a lead but something is missing: the post is old or has no verified date, only part of it matches, or your area is not confirmed. Look before you act.
  • Not a lead (never shown as a lead): someone advertising their own service, a definition or how-to question, a job seeker, news, or small talk. They are listed under "excluded" with the reason.

Older posts are labelled historical: they show what people asked in the past, not who is asking now. Flintel never contacts anyone for you.
