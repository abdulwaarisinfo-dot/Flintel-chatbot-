Joining and Leaving War changes:    AI empires are considerably less willing to join ongoing wars. They now weigh the size of the war, whether they are already fighting other wars, whether they have any quarrel with the opposing war leader, and how they feel about the empire that is asking.   When deciding whether or not to join a war, an empire's opinion of each side is now averaged across that side rather than summed, so a war is no longer more tempting to join just because there are many empires in it.   Bribing a reluctant empire into a war costs substantially more, and each reason they would rather stay out raises the price further. Empires are more easily talked into defending than into attacking.   AI empires no longer offer to join wars they do not actually want to fight.   Coalitions against a galactic crisis are unaffected by the new reluctance, and still form as readily as before.
