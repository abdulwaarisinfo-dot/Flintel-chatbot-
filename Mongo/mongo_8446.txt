It's physics being patient.\n\n—\n\nLet's talk about why this particular skill is disappearing faster than most people in offices realize.\n\nAutomation has eaten enormous chunks of manufacturing. Not because nobody's tried. Because the variability of real industrial sites keeps breaking the automation.\n\nThere is something almost defiant about that.
